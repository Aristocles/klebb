Please set up a dashboard for someone training for endurance (running
or cycling). I'd like four cards; ask me follow-ups as needed and
propose the manifests before creating anything.

1. **Sessions card** — a log of my cardio sessions. Ask me:
   - Running, cycling, or both (if both, one card with an activity
     field beats two cards)
   - What I want per entry: distance, duration, and average HR are the
     usual trio; offer perceived effort (1-10) and a free-text note as
     optional extras
   - Units (km or mi — remember the answer for the goal card)

   Probably a generic-card with multiple entries per day allowed, but
   pick the shape that fits my answers.

2. **Weekly distance goal** — a rings card that sums my logged
   distance across the week (Mon-Sun) against a weekly target. Ask me
   the target. Drive it from the sessions card's distance field.

3. **Resting heart rate** — daily resting HR, line-chart trend, falling
   is good. If I use an Apple Watch with the Health Auto Export app,
   use the imported template variant instead of a manual-entry card.

4. **HRV** — same deal as resting HR but rising is good. Same
   Apple-Health question applies.

Ask the Apple Health question once, not per card. If I say I log
manually, keep entry friction low: number inputs, no required fields
beyond the value itself.

Do not add pace/split analysis, training-plan scheduling, or any other
cards unless I ask.
