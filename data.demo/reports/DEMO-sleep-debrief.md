## Month at a glance

- **Average duration:** 7.4 hours/night (goal: 8.0)
- **Longest streak at or above goal:** 5 nights
- **Most disruptive night:** 5.2 hours with 3 wake-ups
- **Quality trend:** improving week-on-week (week 1: 3.1, week 4: 3.9 on a 1-5 scale)

## Patterns worth noting

1. **Caffeine after 14:00 correlates with -0.7 h sleep.** Of the six worst-duration nights, five followed a day with two or more afternoon coffees.
2. **Exercise days track +0.4 on sleep quality** but not on duration.
3. **Weekend catch-up sleep** reliably +1.1 h on Friday and Saturday nights vs midweek.

## Recommendations (hypothetical)

- Cap caffeine at 14:00 on weekdays.
- Stack cardio on Wednesday evenings and see if Thursday sleep improves.
- Wind down 30 minutes earlier on Sundays to buffer the Monday dip.
